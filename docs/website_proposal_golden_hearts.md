## 1. Overview

Golden Hearts Premier Living Assistance LLC needs a modern, elegant website that builds trust with families seeking in-home care for their loved ones in Texas. This proposal covers a clean 5-page site - Home, About, Services, Contact, and a Request Consultation form - designed to convert visitors into consultation requests while presenting your brand as caring, professional, and dependable.

## 3. Design & User Experience Approach

- **Modern & elegant:** clean typography, warm color palette suited to senior care (calming blues / warm neutrals), generous white space, professional photography style.
- **Mobile-first responsive:** most families browse on phones - the site must look and work perfectly on mobile, tablet, and desktop.
- **Accessibility (WCAG 2.1):** large readable text, high-contrast colors, keyboard-navigable, screen-reader friendly. This directly serves your senior and disabled audience and is an important trust signal.
- **Clear calls to action:** every page leads visitors toward "Request Consultation" or "Call Us" - designed to convert.
- **Fast loading:** optimized images and lightweight code for a smooth experience.
